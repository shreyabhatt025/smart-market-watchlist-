Smart Market Watchlist is a smarter way to keep track of the stocks you care about.

A normal watchlist shows you the stocks you're following, but when there are many stocks, it can be difficult to know which ones actually need your attention. This project focuses on solving that problem.

Instead of making users scan the entire watchlist, Smart Market Watchlist highlights the stocks where something meaningful has changed since the user's last check. It ranks these changes using an Attention Score and shows the signals and context behind the score.

The experience is built around:

WATCH → DETECT → PRIORITIZE → EXPLAIN → ACKNOWLEDGE

One important part of the project is transparency. The application shows observable signals and context rather than pretending to know the exact reason behind a market movement.

It is not designed to give buy or sell recommendations. The idea is simple: help users quickly understand what changed, so they can decide for themselves what deserves a closer look.

In short, a normal watchlist tells you what you're watching. Smart Market Watchlist helps you understand what deserves your attention.
